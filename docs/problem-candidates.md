# Problem candidates

These are our proposals, not topics prescribed by the lecturer. Funding has a timeline-status checker with four previously passing tests and an offline probe with a checked normal output and three passing synthetic error-case tests. Complete candidate workflows remain unimplemented. Codex helped draft this note; human review is pending.

## 1. Funding preparation — preferred

- **Users:** Voluntary organisations in Innlandet.
- **First scope:** One scheme, Regional Culture Fund.
- **Source:** [Regional Culture Fund, Innlandet](https://innlandetfylke.no/tjenester/sok-tilskudd/kunst-og-kultur/regionalt-kulturfond-innlandet.62589.aspx).
- **Success:** Produce a preparation checklist whose requirements and dates match approved source excerpts, with missing information clearly marked.
- **Limit:** It cannot guarantee eligibility or funding.

## 2. University feedback follow-up — alternative

- **Users:** Students and student representatives at INN.
- **Source:** [NOKUT report on INN quality work, 2026](https://www.nokut.no/globalassets/nokut/rapporter/tilsyn-kvalitetsarbeid/2026/inn_periodisk-tilsyn-kvalitetsarbeid_2026.pdf).
- **Success:** Link each reported issue and follow-up status to supplied evidence, marking completion only when documented.
- **Limit:** Suitable recurring input documents are not yet verified.

## 3. Accessible public notices — alternative

- **Users:** Local public-information editors and readers.
- **Source:** [Accessibility statement](https://uustatus.no/nb/erklaringer/publisert/cc891f86-c607-4331-94b0-77f5ccec897a).
- **Success:** Produce accessible HTML preserving all required source facts and passing the selected automated checks.
- **Limit:** Comprehension and full accessibility need human review.
